IFD tag structure:

1. Tag: 2 byte number. (UInt16)

2. Type:
    * 1 = Byte (UInt8)

    * 2 = ASCII (Char). For strings, null-terminated.
      (Count includes NULL)

    * 3 = Short (UInt16)

    * 4 = Long (UInt32)

    * 5 = Rational, 2 UInt32. Word 1 / Word 2, for a decimal.

    * 7 = Undefined (effectively bytes again)

    * 9 = Slong (2s compliment signed integer. Functionally, Int32)

    * 10 = SRational, 2 Int32. Word 1 / Word 2

    * 129 = UTF-8 String, null terminated (count includes Null)

Count: Number of objects, not necessarily bytes.
